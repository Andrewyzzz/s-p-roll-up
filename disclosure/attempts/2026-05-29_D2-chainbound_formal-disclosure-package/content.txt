────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SUMMARY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The Bolt preconfirmation design — like all inclusion-based bonded preconf
systems — provides ordering and inclusion commitments backed by an
EigenLayer AVS slashing mechanism. This is an A4 mechanism (economic
compensation for violation) rather than an A2 mechanism (execution
atomicity guarantee).

The PEO attack applies to any such system:

  ΔΠ = max(δ - bond_penalty × fee_multiplier, 0)

where δ is the marginal MEV value of an alternative block composition.
For any power-law tail MEV distribution, Pr[δ > bond] > 0 for any finite
bond, so the expected violation rate is bounded below by ρ_min > 0
regardless of bond size.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
APPLICABILITY TO BOLT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Bolt's commitment model:
  - Proposers sign commitments to include specific transactions (inclusion only).
  - EigenLayer restaking backs the commitment with slashable collateral.
  - Violation = non-inclusion → slashing.

This is structurally identical to the mev-commit case (see D2_mevcommit
disclosure). The key structural fact: the commitment covers INCLUSION, not
execution outcome. The proposer holds a free option to violate whenever the
alternative block composition is worth more than the slash penalty.
