\subsection{Disclosure Timeline and Correspondence}
\label{app:disclosure}

\paragraph{Blind-review notice.}
Specific disclosure dates, vendor correspondence, OpenTimestamps proof
hashes, and git commit identifiers are withheld in this submission to
preserve author anonymity. The complete, independently verifiable disclosure
record---including per-attempt OTS proofs anchored to the Bitcoin
blockchain and git commit timestamps---will be released in full upon
acceptance as part of the artifact package~\cite{artifact-repo}.

\paragraph{Summary.}
All disclosures in Table~\ref{tab:vendor-responses} (\S\ref{sec:disclosure})
were made at least 90 days prior to submission. The two-layer timestamping
scheme (\S\ref{sec:disclosure-timestamps}) ensures that disclosure content
is tamper-evidently fixed before the submission date.

\paragraph{Structure of the disclosure record.}
Each entry in \texttt{disclosure/disclosure\_commitments.json} contains:
\texttt{vendor}, \texttt{category} (D1/D2), \texttt{date},
\texttt{channel}, \texttt{git\_commit\_hash},
\texttt{opentimestamps\_proof} (path to .ots file),
\texttt{content\_sha256}, \texttt{response\_date}, \texttt{response\_summary}.

\paragraph{Verification at camera-ready.}
To verify a disclosure attempt: \texttt{ots verify content.txt.ots}
(returns Bitcoin block attestation); \texttt{shasum -a 256 content.txt}
(matches SHA-256 in \texttt{disclosure\_commitments.json}).
