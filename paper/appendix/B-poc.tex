\subsection*{B.1 PEO Attack (\texttt{poc/peo/})}

Foundry reimplementation of mev-commit commitment architecture.
Contracts: \texttt{PreconfManager.sol}, \texttt{ProviderRegistry.sol},
\texttt{BidderRegistry.sol}, \texttt{MockOracle.sol}.

Four tests, all passing: (1)~\texttt{testPEO\_ViolationProfit}:
$B=1$\,ETH, $s=0.05$\,ETH, $\delta=0.12$\,ETH $\Rightarrow$ net
$+0.0675$\,ETH; (2)~bond depletion at 19 violations;
(3)~adversarial low $s$ amplification; (4)~honest baseline.

\verb|cd poc/peo && forge test -vv|

\subsection*{B.2 PEFO Structural Demonstration (\texttt{poc/pefo-devnet/})}

\texttt{pefo\_exploit\_demo.py}: confirms C1/C2/C3 on Astria local devnet;
measures 2,199\,ms PEFO window (\S\ref{sec:meas-timing}); computes
$\Delta\Pi^{\text{PEFO}} = 0.08$\,ETH.

\texttt{fault\_inject\_demo.py}: Monte Carlo $N=10{,}000$; PEFO feasible
under attacker delays up to 1,500\,ms, partial failure probability
$p \leq 1$, and sequencer jitter $\sigma \leq 500$\,ms
(\S\ref{sec:meas-faultinject}).

\begin{verbatim}
~/bin/astria-go dev run --instance rollup-a --network local --headless
python3 poc/pefo-devnet/pefo_exploit_demo.py
\end{verbatim}
